INVESTIGATION TRAINING

Screen Share

Screen Share (SS) is a sensitive review process. It must be transparent, limited in scope and respectful of the player's rights.

IMPORTANT: EXPLICIT PERMISSION IS REQUIRED

Every step must be performed only with the person's informed, explicit and voluntary permission. Explain what will be reviewed, why it is necessary, what tools will be used and how the recording will be handled. The person may refuse or stop the session. Never force access, use stealth access or continue after permission is withdrawn.

1. Recording & Transparency

If everyone has agreed, the staff member may record the review with an approved recording tool such as OBS or Bandicam. Tell the participant when recording starts and ends. Recordings should be stored securely, shared only with authorized staff and deleted according to server policy.

2. AnyDesk & Remote Sessions

AnyDesk is a remote-support application. It must never be used to secretly access a player's device. A session requires clear permission, visible participation, a limited purpose and a clear end point. Do not ask for passwords or access to unrelated applications.

3. Minecraft Version & Game Information

Game version information, visible settings and the F3 debug display can provide context about the current game session. They are not conclusive proof of cheating. Record what was voluntarily shown and compare it with the server's rules and other reliable evidence.

4. Mods & Client Information

A player may voluntarily show relevant Minecraft mod or client information. Compare names and versions with the server's current policy. A filename or unfamiliar modification is not automatically proof of cheating. Consider legitimate mods, dependencies and false positives.

5. Windows Activity & Temporary Data

Windows activity records, recent-item lists, temporary data and timestamps can be incomplete, misleading or unrelated to a game session. Do not browse personal folders, private documents, photos, messages or unrelated history. Do not treat a timestamp as proof on its own.

6. Process Information

Process-monitoring tools may show that applications are running and may provide general technical context. Process names and start times do not prove that a cheat was used. Avoid invasive memory extraction or searching a person's private data.

7. Strings, Memory & Technical Indicators

Memory strings and technical indicators can be ambiguous, incomplete or generated by legitimate software. This academy does not provide procedures for extracting memory contents or searching private device data. Any technical finding must be independently validated and reviewed by authorized staff.

8. JNA & JNativeHook

Some legitimate applications and mods use libraries such as JNA or JNativeHook. Their presence alone does not establish cheating. Check the software context and avoid automatic punishment based on a single library name.

9. Evidence & Final Decision

Document the time, scope, participant's consent, observations and relevant evidence. Separate facts from assumptions. If evidence is uncertain, stop and escalate to a senior reviewer. Give the player an opportunity to explain and appeal under server policy.

Privacy boundary: Never inspect unrelated personal files, messages, photos, passwords, accounts or private browsing content. Never force a remote session. If permission is not given or is withdrawn, stop the review.
